Free data assessment

Free data and reporting assessment

A fixed-scope review of one data area that ends with a working prototype on your own data. You get a clear picture of where reporting breaks today, a step-by-step plan to fix it, and a prototype you can try, at no cost.

Request the assessmentDownload the approach document (PDF)

What you get

Audit and gap analysis

A review of your current data workflows to find manual bottlenecks, Excel dependencies and single points of failure in reporting.

Modernisation roadmap

A step-by-step plan to move from manual reporting to automated, current dashboards and plain-English questions.

Working prototype

A prototype on your data: usually AskData answering your top questions, or a dashboard showing how your KPIs will look.

Business impact summary

Where time could be saved, where errors come from, and what it would take to scale, measured against your own baseline.

How it runs

  1. Scope

    Agree one data area, your top questions and success criteria.

  2. Review

    Read-only look at the sources, definitions and current reports.

  3. Prototype

    Build a working prototype on your data.

  4. Present

    Findings, roadmap and prototype; you decide on next steps.
