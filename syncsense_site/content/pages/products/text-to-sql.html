AskData: Text-to-SQL that business users can trust

AskData lets business users ask questions of your own databases in plain English and get a table, a chart and a short summary in seconds. It runs inside your network, accepts only read-only queries and applies each person’s access rules to every answer. It is for finance, sales, operations and leadership teams that wait days for data today.

  • CFO and finance: faster closes and variance answers, monthly packs that send themselves, one definition of revenue.
  • COO and operations: plant, inventory and delivery questions answered on the floor.
  • Sales and marketing: customer, product and territory performance, with forecasts and segments.
  • CIO and data office: self-service without losing control: read-only, governed, audited, your choice of AI.

Problems it solves

  • Every new business question becomes a ticket in the analytics backlog.
  • Dashboards answer last quarter’s questions; the follow-up “why?” needs another request.
  • Different teams have different spreadsheets and different definitions of “revenue”.
  • Security teams block AI tools that would send company data outside.

How does AskData work?

The key design idea: the AI model only writes SQL. Checking, running, charting and summarising happen in AskData, next to your data.

  1. Ask

    Pick a data area and type a question in your own words. One click on Rephrase tidies a vague question first.

  2. Generate and check

    The AI model writes one SELECT. AskData parses it, refuses anything that is not a single read-only query, and adds your row filters and masking.

  3. Run and explain

    The query runs on your database. AskData builds the chart and a plain-language summary of what was counted, for which period and with which filters.

  4. Learn

    Users confirm or report answers. A data steward fixes reported ones and records the rule, so the next person gets it right.

Security and deployment

  • Runs in your environment. One server process or containers on PostgreSQL; can run offline. Not offered as SaaS.
  • The AI never sees your numbers. It receives the question and table descriptions only. Results, summaries and report contents never go to the model.
  • Read-only in depth. SQL guard, read-only sessions, statement timeouts and row caps. We also recommend a database account with SELECT-only grants.
  • Per-person rules. Row-level filters and masked columns apply to chats, shared items, reports and scheduled emails. Shared content re-runs with the viewer’s own access.
  • Sign-in and audit. Two-step sign-in, roles (analyst, steward, admin) and an audit log of every sign-in, question and change.
