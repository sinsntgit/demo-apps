Product · Text-to-SQL

AskData: Text-to-SQL that business users can trust

Business users ask questions of your own databases in plain English and get a table, a chart and a short summary in seconds. AskData runs inside your network, only reads data, and applies each person’s access rules to every answer.

Answers in seconds

Questions in your own words; a table, chart and summary come back straight away.

Checked and correctable

Every answer says what was counted. Users report errors; a data steward fixes them for everyone.

Governed access

Read-only queries, row-level rules and masked fields on every answer, report and share.

Your data stays put

The AI sees the question and table names only. Results never leave your network.

How it works

  1. Ask

    Pick a data area and type a question.

  2. Check

    The AI writes one read-only query; AskData checks it and applies your access rules.

  3. Answer

    The query runs on your database; AskData draws the chart and summary.

  4. Learn

    Confirmed answers become examples; reported ones are fixed by a steward.

Try a sample question

Pick a question to see the SQL AskData would write and the result. Runs on made-up sample data in your browser.

Reports, forecasts and scheduled emails are built from the same answers.

Works with

PostgreSQL and Excel/CSV are verified end to end. SQL Server, Oracle, MySQL, SingleStore and Snowflake use standard connectors and are confirmed on your systems in the pilot. SAP works through replicated tables.

AI models: Claude, OpenAI, or a self-hosted model such as Ollama or vLLM.

Security and deployment

  • Runs on your servers or cloud; not sold as SaaS.
  • Single read-only query only; database grants as a second safeguard.
  • Two-step sign-in, roles and a full audit log.
  • Four-week pilot on your own data before you decide.
