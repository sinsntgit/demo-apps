Product · CRM

Ovantaa CRM: from first lead to cash received

Ovantaa CRM is one platform for leads, deals, email campaigns, GST-ready invoicing and collections, on a single customer record. It is built for small and mid-size B2B teams in India and the GCC, and runs in your own environment so customer data stays with you.

{{booking_link}}Ask about Ovantaa CRM

Who it is for

  • Small and mid-size B2B sales teams that invoice their customers.
  • Companies in India and the GCC that need GST, ZATCA or VAT-correct invoices.
  • Teams outgrowing spreadsheets that want one system, configured quickly.
  • Businesses that want customer data kept in their own environment.

Not the best fit today for very large seat counts, native mobile apps, built-in telephony, complex CPQ, or teams that want a vendor-hosted service.

Problems it solves

  • Re-typing between systems: deals close in the CRM and the same details are keyed again into invoicing software.
  • Compliance by memory: GSTIN checks, CGST/SGST versus IGST and place of supply depend on whoever invoices that day.
  • Forecasts by spreadsheet: monthly and quarterly reviews are assembled by hand from exports.
  • Cost per seat: subscriptions add up with every hire, and customer data sits in someone else’s cloud.

How does Ovantaa CRM work?

One thread from first lead to cash received, on the same customer record and audit trail.

  1. Capture and convert

    Leads are captured or imported, then converted into an account and a deal in one step, with no re-typing.

  2. Sell

    Move deals on a board; every stage change is recorded with who, when and why. Files live in the stage where they matter.

  3. Invoice

    Create a GST-correct invoice from the deal and the account, checked as you type, and email it with a payment link.

  4. Collect and review

    Reminders, payments and receipts, then a business review pack with pipeline, forecast scenarios and cash in.

Key features

Sell

Contacts and leads, accounts with a 360° view, a drag-and-drop deal board with stage history, tasks with email assignment, files with an in-app viewer, and duplicate merging.

Market

Rule-based segments, templates with merge tags, an inbox check before sending, and campaigns sent from your own mailboxes with per-recipient tracking.

Bill and collect

India GST by default, with Saudi ZATCA, UAE and other countries as a setting. Payment pages, automatic reminders, collection tasks, ageing and an 8-week cash-in forecast.

Insight

A dashboard in rupees with Indian grouping, a business review for monthly and quarterly meetings with worst, projected and best case, and two currencies on deals.

AI search

Ask “open deals over 5 lakh closing this quarter”. Only the question and field names go to the AI; the search runs inside your database with your permissions.

Control

Roles and permissions, two-factor sign-in, an audit log, custom fields and form layouts, and safe editing when several people work on one record.

Where it fits with our other products

Ovantaa CRM is the customer-facing layer. Its data sits in your own database, so AskData can answer questions about pipeline, invoices and collections in plain English, and the Ontology Studio can link the CRM’s customer record to the same customer in your other systems.

Security and deployment

  • Runs in your environment; customer data stays with you.
  • Every API endpoint is permission-classified and tested: 175 endpoints, 127 automated tests, plus a browser end-to-end test for every release.
