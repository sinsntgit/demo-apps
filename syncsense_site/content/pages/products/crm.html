Product · CRM

Ovantaa CRM: from first lead to cash received

One platform for leads, deals, email campaigns, GST-ready invoicing and collections, on a single customer record. Built for small and mid-size B2B teams in India and the GCC, and run in your own environment.

Lead, deal, invoice and payment share one customer record.

Reminders, collection tasks, ageing and an 8-week cash-in forecast.

Your data, your servers

Self-hosted, with roles, two-factor sign-in and an audit trail.

How it works

  1. Capture

    Import or add leads; convert to an account and deal in one step.

  2. Sell

    Move deals on a board; every stage change is recorded.

  3. Invoice

    Raise a GST invoice from the deal and email it with a payment link.

  4. Collect and review

    Reminders and receipts, then a monthly review with forecast scenarios.

Good fit

Distributors, manufacturers, IT and professional services firms and contractors that sell to and invoice other businesses, in India and the GCC (Saudi ZATCA and UAE rule-sets included).

Not the best fit today for thousands of seats, native mobile apps, built-in telephony, complex CPQ or a vendor-hosted service.

Security and deployment

  • Runs in your environment; customer data stays with you.
  • AI search sends only the question and field names, never records.
  • Issued invoices cannot be altered; append-only audit trail.
  • 127 automated tests and a browser test for every release.
