Products

AI data products that give business users trusted answers

SyncSense builds three products that work on their own or together: AskData answers questions in plain English, the Ontology Studio gives the business one shared model of its data, and Ovantaa CRM runs the customer-facing work from lead to cash. All three run in your own environment.

How do the products fit together?

The Ontology Studio describes what a “customer”, “order”, “policy” or “subscriber” means and how they relate across your systems. AskData lets business users ask questions in plain English; it is more accurate when it sits on that shared vocabulary. Ovantaa CRM puts customer data in front of sales and service teams. Consulting designs and implements it, and staffing provides people to run and extend it.
