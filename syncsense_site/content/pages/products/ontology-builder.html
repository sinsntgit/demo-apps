Product · Ontology & knowledge graph

Ontology Studio: one shared model of your business data

Ontology Studio builds a connected model of what your business terms mean and how they relate, then fills it with your records and documents as a knowledge graph. Reports, analytics and AI then use the same definitions, and AI answers can cite their sources.

Lineage you can show

Every fact keeps a link to the system or document it came from.

How it works

  1. Collect sources

    Connect systems and documents.

  2. Define the model

    Classes, relations and properties, starting from a library ontology.

  3. Build the graph

    Records and passages loaded with metadata such as source, date and status.

  4. Answer with evidence

    Queries follow the graph and cite the document or event behind each claim.

Start from a pre-built library

22 ready-made ontologies across 19 industries, each built on recognised standards: FIBO for financial services, HL7 FHIR and SNOMED CT for healthcare, IEC CIM for utilities, and more. Copy one into your library and extend it for your business.
