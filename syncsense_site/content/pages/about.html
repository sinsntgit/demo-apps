About

About SyncSense

SyncSense is a data and AI company based in Mumbai, India. We build AI products that give business users trusted answers from their own data, and we provide the consulting and staffing to make them work. Our team brings 25+ years of combined experience in enterprise data, BI and data integration.

Our team

SyncSense was founded by a group of data and analytics professionals with a shared goal: bring enterprise-grade technology and reporting capabilities to businesses at lower cost and faster time to market. The team’s background spans organisations such as SAS, Oracle, Deloitte, BDO and leading analytics firms, and together we have executed 10+ projects.

Every engagement is led by a senior architect who stays involved from the first workshop to production. Follow SyncSense on LinkedIn or watch our product demos on YouTube.

What we believe

Reporting should not be a monthly pain point or a black box. Insights should be accessible, explainable and owned by business users, while data governance stays strong.

We combine a working understanding of finance, risk and operations with hands-on implementation experience on data and BI platforms, so solutions are technically sound and useful in practice.

We are open about limits. Our product material says which databases are verified and which are confirmed during a pilot, and we do not promise accuracy figures before measuring them on your data.

Where we work

We are based in Mumbai and work with clients across India, the Middle East, Africa and the UK, with a mix of onsite and remote delivery.

Typical clients include financial services groups, insurers, telecom operators, tax and professional services firms, technology companies and manufacturers building or scaling analytics, reporting and AI.
