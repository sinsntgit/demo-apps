Services · Staffing

Contract staffing: technical, functional and AI specialists

SyncSense provides vetted technical, functional and AI specialists on contract, either for a defined project or on demand: data engineers and BI developers, finance, risk and supply-chain consultants, and AI engineers. Our experienced architects manage the work, so you get skilled people and someone accountable for the outcome.

Ways to work with us

Project-based staffing

A specialised team assembled for your project milestones and deliverables, from initial scope to launch.

Talent on demand

Pre-vetted professionals who start quickly, to bridge skill gaps and handle fluctuating workloads inside your team.

Managed teams and offshore delivery

A team that runs a function for you, such as BI operations, with recruitment, logistics and day-to-day management handled by us.

Roles we provide

Technical

  • Data engineers (pipelines, ETL/ELT, data warehousing)
  • BI developers (Power BI and other BI tools, SAS reporting)
  • Data scientists and data analysts
  • Solution architects and full-stack developers
  • Data management and governance specialists

Functional

  • Finance and accounting consultants (MIS, consolidation, FP&A)
  • Risk and regulatory reporting specialists
  • Actuarial analysts
  • Supply chain, procurement and sales operations analysts
  • Business analysts who turn requirements into data and BI specifications

AI

  • AI and machine learning engineers
  • LLM and prompt engineers
  • MLOps engineers to run and monitor models in production
  • Forward deployed engineers who build AI systems inside your team

How it works

  1. Tell us the need

    Roles, skills, duration and whether the work is onsite or remote.

  2. Meet vetted people

    Our architects assess candidates technically before you meet them.

  3. Start and review

    People join your team; our architects stay involved and review progress with you.
