Security & privacy

Your data stays where it is

Where the data stays

Our products run in your environment, on your servers or your cloud account. With AskData, the AI model receives only the question and a description of the relevant tables. Query results, customer names and figures stay with you.

Read-only and access-controlled

AskData accepts only a single read-only query, applies each person’s row-level rules and masked fields to every answer, report and shared chat, and logs every question.

Your choice of AI model

Use a provider you already have a contract with, or a model hosted on your own servers, so that not even table names leave your network.

No training on your data

We do not use client data to train models. What AskData learns (your definitions and verified example questions) stays in your installation.
