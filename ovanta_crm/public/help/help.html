Getting started

Ovantaa CRM keeps your customers, deals, tasks, email campaigns and invoices in one place. It runs on one computer or server in your company; everyone else uses it from a web browser.

Signing in

Open the address your administrator gave you (for example http://crm-server:3000) and sign in with your email and password. If your company requires two-factor sign-in you are asked for a 6-digit code from your authenticator app, or taken through setting one up the first time — see Security & two-factor sign-in.

Light and dark mode

Settings → Appearance → My display mode lets you choose light, dark or “match my device” for your own browser.

Dashboard

The dashboard shows how the business is doing at a glance:

  • Contacts, accounts, open pipeline (value of open deals and the probability-weighted forecast), closed won and win rate, and open / overdue tasks.
  • Won revenue for the last six months, pipeline by stage, top open deals, tasks due, the leaderboard, recent activity and recent campaigns.
  • If you use two currencies, totals are shown in both.

People whose role only lets them see their own records see a dashboard of their own records.

AI search

Ask in plain words and get the matching deals, contacts, accounts, files, campaigns and segments — including your custom fields. Open it from the menu or with Ctrl+K.

Questions that work well

  • “Open deals over 50,000 closing this quarter”
  • “Proposal PDFs uploaded in the last 30 days”
  • “My deals stuck in negotiation”
  • “Customers in Riyadh with no phone number”
  • “Accounts with more than 3 open deals”
  • “Partner deals with margin above 10%” (custom fields work too)
  • “Everything about Acme”

Click a file in the results to open it in the viewer; click anything else to open the record.

AI or keyword

AI search needs an Anthropic API key (Settings → AI assistant). Without one — or if the AI is unreachable — it falls back to a keyword search across everything; you can also pick Keyword yourself. See What the AI sees: only your question and field names are sent, never your records.

Contacts

People you sell to or work with. A contact usually belongs to an account (their company).

Everyday tasks

Importing contacts

Import CSV accepts CSV, TSV, TXT and Excel (.xlsx) files up to 5,000 rows. Columns are matched automatically (you can change the mapping), and you choose whether existing contacts (matched by email) are updated or skipped. Download the sample template if you are unsure of the layout. Old .xls files: save them as .xlsx or CSV first.

Converting a lead

When a lead is ready to buy, open it and click Convert lead. In one step you can link it to an existing account or create a new one (accounts whose website matches the person's email domain are suggested), open a deal, and mark the contact Qualified or Customer. The contact then shows Converted ✓ and links to the deal.

Accounts

Companies you sell to. An account page shows its contacts, deals, won value, activity, invoices and files.

  • For invoicing, fill in the billing email, address, State / province (used as the GST place of supply in India) and the customer's tax ID (VAT number, GSTIN, TRN…).
  • Missing or invalid customer details are listed on E-invoicing readiness.

Duplicates and merging

Duplicates lists contacts with the same email or name and accounts with the same name or VAT number.

  1. Pick the record to keep in each group.
  2. Click Merge into the kept record.

Deals, tasks, notes, invoices, files and campaign history move to the kept record; its empty fields are filled from the others; tags are combined; an unsubscribed contact stays unsubscribed. Merging cannot be undone. It needs the Merge duplicates permission.

Deals and the pipeline

A deal is a sales opportunity moving through stages: Prospecting → Qualification → Proposal → Negotiation → Closed Won / Closed Lost.

Creating a deal

+ New deal. The form is the layout your administrator saved; custom fields may appear or hide depending on other answers (for example Partner margin only for Partner deals).

Changing stage

Drag the card on the board, or click Change stage on the deal page. Add a comment (why it moved) and, optionally, attach files to the new stage's folder — for example the signed proposal.

Stage history

Every move is kept permanently: from → to, when, who moved it, who owned the deal then, its value, the comment, and how long it spent in the previous stage. The files of each stage are linked under it. Open it on the deal page or with 🕘 History on any card.

List view

Click List to see deals as a table you can sort and filter, including custom fields shown in lists and the amount in your second currency.

Two currencies on deals

Deals are entered in Currency 1 and also shown in Currency 2, converted with a rate per year. Set it up in Settings → Deal currencies (administrators).

  1. Choose Currency 1 (what amounts are typed in) and Currency 2, then Save currencies.
  2. + Add year and enter how many units of Currency 2 one unit of Currency 1 is worth, e.g. 2026: 1 USD = 84.20 INR.

Which year's rate a deal uses

  • Open deals: the year of the expected close date, or of the created date if there is no close date.
  • Won or lost deals: the year they were closed.
  • No rate for that year → the latest earlier year is used, and the deal page says so. No rate at all → not converted.

Amounts are converted live: correcting a year's rate updates every deal of that year. Every change is in the change history. Both currencies appear on the deal page, the deals list, board cards and totals, the dashboard and AI search. Invoices are not affected — they have their own currency.

Files, stage folders and the viewer

Every contact, account, deal, task and invoice has a Files card. Add up to 20 files at a time by dragging them onto the card or with + Add files (25 MB each by default). Programs and scripts (.exe, .js, .bat…) are refused.

Stage folders on deals

  • New files go into the current stage's folder unless you choose another in “to …”.
  • Move a file to another folder with the drop-down on its row.
  • Files are stored the same way on disk: data/files/deals/<deal id>/<stage>/.

Viewing files

Click a file to view it without downloading: PDFs, images, video and audio, text and CSV, and a preview of Excel (.xlsx, first sheet) and Word (.docx, text). The arrows step through the record's files; Copy link copies an in-app link (people still sign in).

Office viewer ↗ opens PowerPoint and other Office files in Microsoft's online viewer using a link that works for one hour. It only works when the CRM is reachable from the internet (BASE_URL set to its public https address) and it sends that file to Microsoft, so it is only used when you click it.

Deleting files

Only administrators can delete files. Everyone else can view, upload, download and move them. Deleting a whole record (for example a deal) also removes its files.

Tasks and assignments

Tasks are assigned to people in your team. The assignee is emailed the task and its due date; issues and completion are emailed back to the person who assigned it.

Assigning a task

+ New task (or + Task on a contact or deal) → choose Assign to, the due date, priority, details and an optional message. When you save, a message tells you who is being emailed and from which address.

Working on a task

Click a task to open it. What you can do depends on whether you are the assignee or the person who assigned it:

WhoActionWho is emailed
AssigneeMark complete (optional note and files)The assigner
AssigneeRaise an issue (description required, files optional)The assigner
EitherReplyThe other person
AssignerResolve & send back / reassign — same or another person, new due date, note, filesThe new assignee (and the previous one if it moved)
EitherReopen a completed task; edit due date, title, priority…The other person (the email lists what changed)
  • Every step is on the task's timeline. A delivery problem shows as ✉️ Email failed with the mail server's message.
  • You are never emailed about your own changes. Tick Email me a copy of my own changes at the top of the Tasks page if you want them.
  • Tasks are only assigned to team members; contacts and deals are just “related to”.
  • If task emails are not arriving, see Troubleshooting.

Working together on the same record

Several people can safely work on the same contact, account, deal or task.

  • See who else is there. Record pages show “👁 Liam is viewing” or “✏️ Liam is editing”.
  • Edits merge. An edit form only saves the fields you changed. If Liam changes a deal's amount while you change its close date, both are kept.
  • Same field? You choose. If someone else changed the same field after you opened the form, nothing is overwritten: you see Was / Their value / Your value for each such field. Pick one and click Save again.
  • Pages refresh themselves when someone else saves — but never while you have a dialog open.

Campaigns, segments and templates

1. Choose who receives it — segments

Segments → + New segment: combine rules such as status, source, tags, owner, account fields and custom fields (all rules, or any rule). The preview shows how many contacts match. Unsubscribed contacts are always excluded.

2. Write it

Campaigns → + New campaign. Pick the sender, segment, subject and content (or start from a template). Personalise with merge tags such as {{first_name|there}} (“there” is used when the first name is empty), {{company}}, {{title}}. ✨ Write with Claude drafts or rewrites the email from a short brief.

3. Test and send

  1. Send test to yourself first.
  2. Send now or Schedule… (needs the Send permission). The Send now dialog asks which email address to send from, and says whether a copy will appear in that mailbox’s Sent folder. The audience is frozen when sending starts.
  3. Emails go out one by one through the chosen sender, respecting its 24-hour limit and pause. If the limit is reached the campaign pauses and continues automatically.

4. Results

Each recipient shows sent, failed (with the server's reason), opened, clicked and unsubscribed. Every email gets an unsubscribe link automatically. Open and click tracking need BASE_URL to be the CRM's public address. Open counts are approximate (some mail apps block images).

Email senders

Every email the CRM sends — campaigns, task emails, notifications, invoices, receipts, reminders — goes out through an email sender: one of your mailboxes. Out of the box only a “Demo sender (simulation)” exists, which sends nothing.

Adding a sender

  1. Email senders → + Add sender.
  2. Pick the provider preset, enter the from name and address, the username (usually the full email address) and the password.
  3. Save, then Verify (checks the login) and Send test.

Seeing sent emails in your mailbox's Sent folder

Emails sent through SMTP do not appear in your Sent folder by themselves (Gmail and Microsoft 365 are exceptions — they file them automatically). To keep a copy, edit the sender and tick Save a copy … in this mailbox’s Sent folder. The presets fill the IMAP server (GoDaddy/Titan: imap.secureserver.net, Titan elsewhere: imap.titan.email, Zoho: imap.zoho.in; port 993); it signs in with the same username and password. The folder marked Sent is found automatically — type its name only if your copies should go elsewhere.

Copies are kept for campaign, invoice, receipt and reminder emails and for Send test. Verify checks the IMAP sign-in and names the folder. If saving a copy fails, the email is still sent and the reason is shown on the sender and in the campaign report. Each recipient adds one message to your mailbox, so keep an eye on its storage for big campaigns.

Which sender is used

  • Campaigns: the sender chosen on the campaign.
  • Invoices and reminders: the one chosen in Billing settings, otherwise the default.
  • Tasks and notifications: the default sender if it is a real mailbox, otherwise the first active real one — never the simulation sender when a real one exists. The first real sender you add becomes the default.

Limits and good practice

  • Each sender has a 24-hour limit and a pause between emails. Mailboxes are not built for bulk mail: Gmail allows about 500/day, GoDaddy often 250. For large campaigns use a transactional provider (Amazon SES, SendGrid, Brevo…).
  • Publish SPF, DKIM and DMARC for your domain (the checklist on the page explains them).
  • Passwords are stored encrypted. Keep data/.secret with your backups — without it saved passwords must be re-entered.

Sender problems (535, ESOCKET, timeouts): see Troubleshooting.

Notifications and the email log

Notifications → Rules email people when something happens: a contact is added, updated, imported or deleted; an account is created; a deal is created, updated, changes stage, is won, lost or deleted.

  • Recipients can be chosen users, the record's owner and/or any email addresses.
  • Optional filters: minimum deal amount, particular stages.
  • Send me a test checks a rule without waiting for a real event.

Email log

The Email log tab lists every notification, task, invoice, receipt, reminder and test email with its status: sent (accepted by the mail server), failed (with the reason) or simulated (nothing was sent — only the simulation sender was available). This is the first place to look when someone says they did not get an email.

Invoicing setup — Billing settings

Choose the country rules your invoices follow, then fill in your business profile and payment details. Until the profile is complete a red banner lists what is missing and invoices cannot be issued.

Invoicing mode

ModeWhat you get
Saudi Arabia — ZATCABilingual tax invoices, 15-digit VAT checks, building/district/postal checks, QR code, hash chain, XML, and optional submission to ZATCA through a certified provider.
United Arab Emirates — VATTRN checks, 5 % VAT, AED, bilingual.
India — GSTGSTIN check, your state and the customer's state decide CGST + SGST (same state) or IGST (another state); exports; HSN/SAC per line; PAN; amount in words; Indian number grouping (1,00,000.00); bank account, IFSC and UPI.
Any other countryPick a country (UK, USA, Thailand, Singapore, Australia, Bahrain, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait, Egypt, Jordan, Germany, France, Netherlands, Ireland, Canada, South Africa, Kenya, Nigeria, Japan, or Other). Currency, tax name, default rate, tax-ID label, invoice title and bank-code label are filled in — all editable. Not tax-registered? Untick it.

Switching mode applies to new invoices; existing invoices keep the rules they were made under.

The other settings

  • Numbering: prefixes for invoices, credit notes and debit notes. Numbers are sequential, with no gaps, never reused. (GST invoice numbers may be at most 16 characters.)
  • Defaults: currency, payment terms (days), default tax rate.
  • Payment details: bank, account name and number, IBAN, bank code (IFSC, sort code, routing number…), SWIFT, UPI. Shown on invoices, emails, reminders and the payment page.
  • Automatic payment reminders: switch on, choose the sender, and set the schedule — days relative to the due date (negative = before) and channels (email, WhatsApp, SMS), then “repeat every N days”. Optionally create a collection task for overdue invoices.
  • WhatsApp / SMS gateway (optional). Without one, WhatsApp reminders become a ready-made WhatsApp message your team sends with one click.
  • E-invoicing provider (KSA/UAE only): connect a certified provider for clearance/reporting.

Creating and issuing invoices

Creating an invoice

  1. + New invoice and choose the customer; their details (address, state, tax ID, billing email) are filled in.
  2. Add the lines: description, HSN/SAC (India), quantity, unit price, discount and tax treatment (standard, zero-rated/export, exempt, out of scope) with the rate.
  3. Check the Compliance check panel on the right: Must fix problems block issuing; Check items are warnings.
  4. Save draft to finish later, or Save & issue.

Issuing

Issuing gives the invoice its number and locks it: issued invoices can never be edited or deleted. To correct one, create a Credit note (to reduce or cancel it — it starts as a copy you can reduce) or a Debit note (to add charges). Both need a reason and reference the original invoice.

Right after issuing you are offered to email the invoice to the customer.

Emailing invoices, recording payments and reminders

Emailing the invoice

✉️ Email invoice opens a pre-filled email: To (the customer's billing email), Cc, subject and message. The email contains a summary, a View invoice & pay button to the customer payment page, your payment details, and the invoice attached as a printable page (the customer opens it in any browser and can use Print → Save as PDF). The invoice then shows ✉️ emailed, and the email is in the email log. Needs the Email & remind permission.

Recording payments

Record payment: amount (part or all of the balance), date, method, bank reference. The status becomes Partially paid or Paid automatically. Tick Email a payment receipt to send the customer a “Payment received — thank you” email with the remaining balance.

  • Reverse a payment recorded by mistake or that bounced (a reason is required). The balance and status are restored and the audit trail keeps both entries.
  • Tick Bank reconciled once the payment is matched with your bank statement.
  • Customers can also tell you they have paid from the payment page: you Confirm received or mark it Not received.

Reminders

Send reminder any time by email, or WhatsApp (opens a ready-made message with the right country code). Automatic reminders follow the schedule in Billing settings; each invoice has a switch to pause or resume them. Reminders include the amount, due date, a pay button and your payment details — in English outside Saudi Arabia and the UAE.

Receivables, readiness and importing invoices

Receivables

Outstanding and overdue amounts, ageing buckets (not yet due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+ days), customers who owe the most, billed versus collected by month, days-sales-outstanding and an 8-week cash-in forecast.

E-invoicing readiness

One list of everything that would stop invoices being issued or accepted: incomplete business profile, customers with missing or invalid tax IDs or addresses, draft invoices with errors, submission problems (KSA/UAE), unreconciled payments, payment notices waiting for confirmation, and tamper checks on the invoice chain. Each item links to where you fix it.

Importing invoices

Invoices → Import from accounting system loads invoices exported from Zoho Books, QuickBooks, Xero or a spreadsheet, maps the columns and checks them against your chosen rules.

Customisation: your own fields and form layouts

Customisation (permission: Customisation). Choose Deals, Accounts or Contacts.

Adding fields

+ Add field: single-line text, comment (multi-line), number, percentage, amount, date, dropdown (type the list of options), multi-select, checkbox, email, phone, web link, or link to another record (e.g. a Partner that points to an account). Each field can be required, have help text and a default, be shown as a list column, be filterable and searchable.

Show only when… hides a field unless another field has certain values — e.g. show Partner margin only when Deal type = Partner. Hidden fields are not required and keep any saved value.

Arranging the form

Group fields into named sections (one or two columns), drag them (or use ↑ ↓), hide optional standard fields, Preview form, and Save layout — everyone then sees that form. Reset to default undoes it.

Removing fields

Archive hides a field everywhere but keeps its data (restore any time). Deleting permanently removes its values from every record. A field's type never changes after creation. Renaming a dropdown option renames it on every record.

Team, roles and permissions

Users

Team & roles → Users → + New user: name, real email address (task and notification emails go there), role and an initial password (at least 8 characters with letters and numbers). Edit a user to change their role, reset their password or deactivate them. Reset 2FA helps someone who lost their phone. There is always at least one active administrator, and you cannot change your own role.

Inviting people by email

Team & roles → Users → + Invite user: enter the person's name, real email and role, and leave Email an invitation link ticked. They receive an email with an Accept invitation button, choose their own password and are signed in. The link works once and expires after 7 days. Until they accept, the list shows invited · pending and a Resend invite button (resending cancels the older link).

After inviting, a dialog tells you whether the email went out and always shows the link with Copy link, so you can also send it by WhatsApp or chat. Untick the box to set a password yourself instead.

Two things must be set for invitation emails to work:

  1. A real email sender under Email senders (not only the simulation one) — otherwise the dialog says Not emailed.
  2. The public address under Settings → Email delivery — the address your colleagues type to open the CRM, e.g. http://crm-server:3000 or http://192.168.1.20:3000. If it says localhost, links only work on the computer running the CRM.

Forgot password

On the sign-in page, Forgot password? emails a reset link (valid 2 hours, works once). Saving a new password signs the person out everywhere else; people with two-factor sign-in still enter their code.

Roles

Besides the three built-in roles you can create your own (or duplicate one). For each area — contacts, accounts, deals, tasks, invoices, campaigns, files… — choose what the role may do. For contacts, accounts, deals, tasks and invoices you choose the scope: own (records they own; for tasks also tasks they assigned) or all.

  • Changes apply immediately, even to people already signed in.
  • You can only grant permissions you hold yourself.
  • The Administrator role always has everything. Deleting attached files is reserved for administrators.
  • The Audit log tab records sign-ins, changes, exports, deletions and settings changes.

Security and two-factor sign-in

Turning on two-factor sign-in

  1. Install an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Authy, 1Password…).
  2. Settings → Security → Set up, scan the QR code (or type the key), enter the 6-digit code and click Turn on.
  3. Save the 10 recovery codes — each lets you sign in once if you lose your phone. They are shown only once.

Administrators can require two-factor for administrators or everyone (Company policy); people who have not set it up are taken through it at their next sign-in.

Signed-in devices

The same card lists where you are signed in; sign out any device or all others. Signing out, changing your password, a 2FA reset or deactivation ends sessions immediately. Sessions also end after 8 hours without activity.

Changing your password

Settings → My account. Changing it signs out your other devices.

Installation and operations

Requirements

  • Node.js 22.5 or newer (node -v). Nothing else: the database (SQLite) is built in.
  • One computer or server that stays on while people work. Everyone else only needs a browser.

Install and start

cd D:\ovanta_crm
npm install        # once, and again after every upgrade
npm start          # opens on http://localhost:3000

The first line in the window shows the version, e.g. Ovantaa CRM v1.20.0 running at …. Colleagues on the same network open http://<computer-name>:3000. To use another port: PowerShell $env:PORT=3001; npm start.

The first start creates data/ovantaa.db with demo data and the accounts admin@ovantaa.com, maya@ovantaa.com, liam@ovantaa.com (password Ovantaa@123). Change these passwords and give users their real email addresses. For a clean start set ADMIN_EMAIL, ADMIN_PASSWORD and SEED_DEMO=0 before the first start.

Upgrading

  1. Stop the server (Ctrl+C in its window).
  2. Copy the new files over the existing folder. Do not delete the data folder. (If the download unpacks into its own ovanta_crm folder, copy that folder's contents across.)
  3. npm install, then npm start. The database is upgraded automatically; a restore point is saved first.
  4. Press Ctrl+F5 in the browser. Check the version under your name.

Backups and restore

  • Back up the whole data folder: the database, backups/, files/ (attachments) and .secret (decrypts saved passwords and API keys).
  • npm run backup takes a verified backup now.
  • Restore: stop the server, then npm run restore (lists backups) and npm run restore -- data/backups/<file>.db --yes. The current database is copied aside first.
  • Copy backups to OneDrive or Google Drive if you like — but do not run the live database from a synced cloud folder.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data kept?

In the data folder on the computer running the CRM: one database file, attachments in data/files, backups in data/backups.

Can I undo a deletion?

Not from the screen. Restore a backup (Installation → Backups and restore) — note this restores everything to that moment.

Why can't I edit an issued invoice?

Tax rules require issued invoices to stay unchanged. Use a credit note to reduce or cancel it, or a debit note to add charges.

Can customers see anything?
